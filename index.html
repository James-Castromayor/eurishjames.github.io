Special Reminders

Weather : February can be quite cool, especially in the evening. We recommend bringing a warmer (jacket, pashmina, or shawl).

Arrival at the Church : As there may be another wedding before ours, arriving closer to the ceremony time is greatly appreciated.

Parking : Ample parking is available in the church and reception venue.

Getting There : For those commuting, the church is conveniently located along the highway. Buses bound for Tagaytay/Nasugbu will pass directly in front of the church.

Accommodations : For those planning to stay overnight, there are several hotels near our venue. For accommodations with a view of Taal Volcano, please expect a scenic 15-minute drive to our location.
